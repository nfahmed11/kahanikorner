Our Story

Kahani Korner was born at 3 a.m. during sleepless baby night shifts, when a quiet realization hit me harder than the exhaustion. The only person I spoke Urdu with was my mom, and unless I started now, Urdu would end with me in my bloodline. At least my parents’ generation had a village. They had people around them who carried the language, even if it was passed down irregularly. My kids do not have that. Their village speaks English. Many have forgotten Urdu, and others are simply uncomfortable using it. I knew I needed to build villagers to supplement the Urdu I was trying to give my kids on my own. That is how Kahani Korner came to life. All the resources in one place. Books, games, music, cartoons, movies, and worksheets that make Urdu feel natural, cozy, and part of everyday family life, one little kahani at a time.

Subscriptions The Kahani Times
What is The Kahani Times?

The Kahani Times is our monthly snail mail subscription: a themed bundle with a bilingual “akhbar” (newspaper-style story/activity), stickers, and hands-on activities designed to make culture and language feel cozy at home.

What comes in the subscription mail?

Each month includes a new theme with a bilingual akhbar (newspaper), stickers, and activities — plus occasional surprise extras.

When will I receive my first snail mail?

Cutoff is the 20th.

Subscribe on 20th or before: first mail ships end of THAT month.

Example: Subscribe Dec 18 → Subscribtion Starts December → ships end of December → arrives early January.

Subscribe after the 20th: your subscription starts the following month.

Example: Subscribe Dec 21 → Subscribtion Starts January → ships end of January → arrives early February.

Can I cancel or pause my subscription?

Yes — you can cancel anytime. If you cancel after the 20th, your next month may already be in production and will be your final mail.

Can I gift a subscription?

Yes! If you’d like a gift note included, contact us after ordering and we’ll help.

What if my mail arrives damaged or goes missing?

Contact us and we’ll make it right.

Books & Language Ages, Urdu, Audio
What age range are the resources for?

Our books are designed for children ages 0–12. The Kahani Times is for any age that wants more cultural exposure through stories, activities, and snail mail.

Do I need to know Urdu to use these?

Not at all. Many families use Kahani Korner even when only one parent speaks Urdu. Everything includes English, Urdu script, and Roman Urdu so anyone can read along — and the QR codes link to audio read-alouds.
