Our Story

Kahani Korner was born from a simple wish: to give children a piece of their heritage they could hold in their hands — and fall in love with.

Why We Started

Growing up between two languages and two cultures is a beautiful, sometimes tricky thing. We wanted to create something that makes the Urdu language feel warm, familiar, and joyful — not like a chore.

Kahani Korner started as an idea on a kitchen table: what if Pakistani–American kids had books, stickers, and stories that looked like them, sounded like them, and made them proud of their heritage?

What We Make

  • Urdu–English picture books — bilingual stories for early readers
  • Cultural stickers — playful designs rooted in South Asian art and joy
  • The Kahani Times — a monthly snail mail subscription for families
  • Free learning resources — songs, cartoons, teacher channels, and a parent guide

Our Mission

We believe language is love. Every book we make, every resource we share, and every snail mail we send is our way of saying: your culture matters, your language matters, and you belong in these pages.

Rooted in culture. Topped with sweetness. Made for little hearts.
