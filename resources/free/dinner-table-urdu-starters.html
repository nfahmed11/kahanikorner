A Sneak Peek

Starter 1

Aaj kya acha hua?

What good thing happened today?

Easy follow-up: Phir kya hua? — Then what happened?

Starter 2

Aaj sab se mazedar cheez kya hui?

What was the funniest or best part of today?

Easy follow-up: Kyun? — Why?

Dinner-Table Rule

English answers still count.

Your child does not have to answer fully in Urdu for the conversation to be working.

Repeat one small part of their answer back in Urdu instead of correcting them.
